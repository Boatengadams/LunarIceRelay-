# Scientific References & Assumptions

## Policy
Do not invent NASA specifications. Prefer supplied Space Apps / NASA materials.  
When gameplay simplifies reality, label it clearly.

## Documented gameplay assumptions
1. **Lunar gravity feel** — rover uses ~1.62 m/s² custom gravity with arcade traction; not a verified vehicle dynamics model.  
2. **CubeSat relay** — communication is abstracted into timed orbit windows + probabilistic interruptions; not Keplerian orbital propagation.  
3. **Battery** — linear Wh drain from drive/idle watts; not a validated electrical power model.  
4. **Ice science** — investigation points and sample scores are educational gameplay markers, not measured deposit maps.
